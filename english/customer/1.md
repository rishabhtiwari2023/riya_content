1. Classic Opening – “Tell me about yourself” (Most important one!)
HR: Good morning/afternoon! Please have a seat. Can you start by telling me something about yourself?
You: Good morning/afternoon! Thank you for having me.
My name is Rishabh, and I recently completed my [degree/course – e.g., B.Tech in Computer Science] from [college name].
During my studies, I focused on [main subjects/projects – e.g., web development, Python programming, and building small apps].
In my final year project, I [brief 1-sentence about project – e.g., developed an e-commerce website using React and Node.js].
Apart from studies, I enjoy [hobby – e.g., playing cricket, reading tech blogs, and learning new skills on YouTube].
Right now, I'm very excited to start my career and contribute to a good company like yours.
HR: Nice to know you. Why do you want to join our company?
(Practice varying it – make it 45–90 seconds long)
2. Strengths & Weaknesses Flow
HR: What are your greatest strengths?
You: I would say three main strengths:
First, I am a quick learner – I can pick up new tools and technologies fast. For example, I learned React.js in just three weeks for my project.
Second, I am good at problem-solving. When I face any issue, I break it into small parts and try different solutions.
Third, I work well in a team. In college group projects, I usually coordinated between members and made sure everyone contributed.
HR: And what about your weaknesses?
You: Honestly, one area I'm working on is public speaking. Earlier I used to feel a bit nervous while presenting in front of large groups.
But now I'm improving it – I have started practicing in front of friends and recording myself. Last month I gave a 10-minute presentation without many pauses, so I'm getting better day by day.
3. Why our company? + Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
HR: Why do you want to work with us?
You: I really admire [Company name] because [genuine reason – e.g., you are a leader in innovative software solutions / you give freshers good learning opportunities / your work culture is very positive according to Glassdoor and LinkedIn reviews].
I want to grow my technical skills in a professional environment, and I believe this company is the perfect place to learn and contribute at the same time.
HR: Where do you see yourself five years from now?
You: In the next 3–5 years, I see myself as a skilled [your field – e.g., full-stack developer / software engineer] who can handle end-to-end project responsibility.
I want to keep learning new technologies, maybe take some certifications, and hopefully take on leadership roles like team lead in the future.
Most importantly, I want to grow along with the company and add real value to the team.

Quick Tips to Improve English Fast

Speak loudly and clearly – even if grammar is not 100% perfect, pronunciation matters more in interviews.
Reduce filler words (umm, ahh, like) → pause for 1 second instead.
Record yourself on phone → listen and note mistakes.
Practice daily 15–20 minutes – rotate 3–4 questions each day.
Use simple sentences first → then add connectors (because, also, for example, however).
