# Business & Office Vocabulary (English to English)

## Professional Communication Terms

**Agenda** - A list of items to be discussed at a meeting
- *Example: Please review the agenda before tomorrow's conference.*

**Deadline** - The latest time or date by which something should be completed
- *Example: The deadline for submitting reports is Friday at 5 PM.*

**Collaborate** - To work jointly with others on an activity or project
- *Example: Our teams will collaborate on the new marketing campaign.*

**Milestone** - A significant stage or event in the development of something
- *Example: Completing the prototype is an important milestone.*

**Stakeholder** - A person with an interest or concern in a business or project
- *Example: We need approval from all stakeholders before proceeding.*

## Action Verbs for Work

**Implement** - To put a plan or system into operation
- *Example: We will implement the new policy next month.*

**Delegate** - To assign responsibility or authority to another person
- *Example: The manager delegated the task to her assistant.*

**Evaluate** - To assess or judge the quality, importance, or value of something
- *Example: We need to evaluate the success of our strategy.*

**Prioritize** - To arrange items in order of importance
- *Example: Learn to prioritize urgent tasks over less important ones.*

**Coordinate** - To organize different elements to work together effectively
- *Example: She coordinates all marketing activities across departments.*

## Meeting & Discussion Terms

**Follow up** - To pursue or investigate something further
- *Example: I'll follow up with you via email after our discussion.*

**Feedback** - Information about reactions to a product or performance
- *Example: We appreciate your feedback on the new system.*

**Action items** - Tasks that need to be completed as a result of a meeting
- *Example: Let's review the action items from today's meeting.*

**Consensus** - General agreement among a group
- *Example: We reached consensus on the budget allocation.*

**Adjourn** - To stop or suspend a meeting until a later time
- *Example: The meeting was adjourned at 4 PM.*

## Email & Communication Terms

**Acknowledge** - To confirm receipt or recognize something
- *Example: Please acknowledge receipt of this email.*

**Clarify** - To make something easier to understand
- *Example: Could you clarify what you mean by "urgent"?*

**Convey** - To communicate or make known
- *Example: This letter will convey our position on the matter.*

**Elaborate** - To explain something in more detail
- *Example: Could you elaborate on your proposal?*

**Correspond** - To communicate by exchanging letters or emails
- *Example: I've been corresponding with the client about their concerns.*

## Performance & Progress Terms

**Benchmark** - A standard or point of reference for measuring progress
- *Example: Our sales exceeded the industry benchmark.*

**Initiative** - An action or strategy intended to resolve a difficulty
- *Example: She took the initiative to improve team communication.*

**Optimize** - To make the best or most effective use of something
- *Example: We need to optimize our workflow for efficiency.*

**Streamline** - To make a process more efficient by simplifying it
- *Example: The new system will streamline our ordering process.*

**Facilitate** - To make an action or process easier
- *Example: Technology can facilitate better communication.*
