# 500 Essential Vocabulary Words (Intermediate to Advanced)
## English to English Definitions with Examples

---

## Section 1: Professional & Business (1-50)

1. **Accomplish** - To successfully complete or achieve something
   - *We accomplished all our goals for the quarter.*

2. **Accurate** - Correct and exact, without errors
   - *Please ensure your report is accurate before submission.*

3. **Achieve** - To successfully reach a goal or complete something
   - *She achieved excellent results in her exams.*

4. **Adapt** - To change or adjust to new conditions
   - *Companies must adapt to changing market conditions.*

5. **Adequate** - Sufficient or enough for a particular purpose
   - *The budget is adequate to cover all expenses.*

6. **Allocate** - To distribute or assign for a specific purpose
   - *We need to allocate resources more efficiently.*

7. **Analyze** - To examine something in detail to understand it
   - *Let's analyze the data before making a decision.*

8. **Anticipate** - To expect or predict something will happen
   - *We anticipate strong sales next quarter.*

9. **Approach** - A way of dealing with a situation or problem
   - *We need a different approach to solve this issue.*

10. **Appropriate** - Suitable or proper for a particular situation
    - *Please use appropriate language in professional emails.*

11. **Approximate** - Close to the actual but not exact
    - *The approximate cost will be $5,000.*

12. **Assess** - To evaluate or judge the quality or value of something
    - *We need to assess the risks before proceeding.*

13. **Assume** - To accept something as true without proof
    - *I assume you've completed the assignment.*

14. **Assure** - To tell someone confidently that something is true
    - *I can assure you that the project will finish on time.*

15. **Attain** - To succeed in achieving something
    - *He attained a senior position in the company.*

16. **Authority** - The power or right to give orders and make decisions
    - *Only managers have the authority to approve expenses.*

17. **Benefit** - An advantage or helpful result
    - *The new policy has many benefits for employees.*

18. **Capable** - Having the ability or skills to do something
    - *She is capable of managing large teams.*

19. **Capacity** - The maximum amount that something can contain or produce
    - *The hall has a capacity of 500 people.*

20. **Circumstance** - A fact or condition connected with an event
    - *Under the circumstances, we made the best decision.*

21. **Colleague** - A person you work with in the same organization
    - *My colleagues are very supportive and helpful.*

22. **Commit** - To dedicate yourself to a particular course of action
    - *We commit to delivering quality service.*

23. **Competent** - Having the necessary skills or knowledge
    - *He is a competent engineer with years of experience.*

24. **Comply** - To obey a rule, law, or request
    - *All employees must comply with safety regulations.*

25. **Comprehend** - To understand something fully
    - *Do you comprehend the complexity of this task?*

26. **Comprehensive** - Complete and including everything
    - *We need a comprehensive plan for the project.*

27. **Comprise** - To consist of or be made up of
    - *The team comprises twelve members from different departments.*

28. **Conceive** - To form an idea or plan in your mind
    - *She conceived the idea during a brainstorming session.*

29. **Concentrate** - To focus all attention on something
    - *Please concentrate on the most important tasks.*

30. **Concept** - An abstract idea or general notion
    - *The concept of sustainability is central to our strategy.*

31. **Conclude** - To bring something to an end or reach a decision
    - *We concluded the meeting with action items.*

32. **Conduct** - To organize and carry out an activity
    - *We will conduct a survey next month.*

33. **Consequence** - A result or effect of an action
    - *Every decision has consequences.*

34. **Considerable** - Large in size, amount, or degree
    - *There has been considerable improvement in sales.*

35. **Consist** - To be made up or composed of
    - *The course consists of ten modules.*

36. **Constant** - Happening all the time or repeatedly
    - *Constant communication is essential for teamwork.*

37. **Constitute** - To form or make up something
    - *These factors constitute a significant risk.*

38. **Constraint** - A limitation or restriction
    - *Budget constraints prevented us from hiring more staff.*

39. **Consult** - To seek advice or information from someone
    - *Please consult the manual before operating the machine.*

40. **Consume** - To use up a resource or eat/drink something
    - *This project consumes a lot of time and resources.*

41. **Contemporary** - Belonging to the present time; modern
    - *Contemporary business practices focus on sustainability.*

42. **Context** - The circumstances or setting in which something occurs
    - *You need to understand the context of the situation.*

43. **Contract** - A formal agreement between parties
    - *We signed a contract with the supplier.*

44. **Contrary** - Opposite in nature or direction
    - *Contrary to expectations, sales increased.*

45. **Contribute** - To give something to help achieve a result
    - *Everyone contributed ideas to the project.*

46. **Conventional** - Based on what is generally done or believed
    - *We're moving away from conventional methods.*

47. **Convert** - To change from one form to another
    - *We need to convert these files to PDF format.*

48. **Cooperate** - To work together toward a common goal
    - *All departments must cooperate to succeed.*

49. **Corporate** - Relating to a large company or corporation
    - *He works in corporate finance.*

50. **Correspond** - To communicate by letters or emails
    - *I correspond regularly with our international clients.*

- **Days 11-20:** Learn 50 words (Section 2) + Review Section 1
- **Days 21-30:** Learn 50 words (Section 3) + Review Sections 1-2
- **Continue pattern for all 10 sections**

### Study Techniques
1. **Read the definition** carefully
2. **Study the example** sentence
3. **Create your own** example sentence
4. **Use flashcards** for daily review
5. **Practice speaking** with these words
6. **Write paragraphs** using new vocabulary

### Weekly Goals
- Learn 100-150 new words
- Review previously learned words
- Use 20-30 words in writing
- Use 10-15 words in speaking

### Retention Tips
- Review words after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days
- Group similar words together
- Learn words in context, not isolation
- Use spaced repetition apps like Anki
